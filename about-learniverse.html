About Us

What is Learniverse?

A digital book platform that makes learning fun, enjoyable, and accessible for children.

What is Learniverse?

Learniverse is a digital book platform designed to make learning fun, enjoyable, and accessible for children and young learners. It brings colorful, easy-to-understand educational books together in one place, so children can explore new ideas, build their reading skills, and discover their creativity.

Parents and guardians can also use Learniverse to find reliable learning materials that support their children’s growth and continued learning at home.

Five Learning Areas

Every book in Learniverse belongs to one of five learning areas.

📚

Language & Reading

Alphabet, vocabulary, grammar, Filipino language, and reading comprehension.

🔢

Mathematics & Logic

Numbers, shapes, puzzles, critical thinking, and money smarts.

🔬

Science & Discovery

Animals, nature, space, dinosaurs, and technology.

🎨

Arts & Creativity

Drawing, music, and cooking for kids.

💛

Life Skills & Values

Good manners, health, feelings, safety, and community helpers.
